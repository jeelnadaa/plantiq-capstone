Google Maps Coordinates Guide

📍 English Instructions:
  1. Open the Google Maps app on your mobile.
  2. Zoom in and long press on your coffee estate plot to drop a red pin.
  3. Look at the top search bar for coordinates like 13.3161, 75.7720.
  4. Enter the first number as Latitude and second as Longitude.
