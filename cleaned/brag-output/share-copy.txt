🌿 Can modern AI solve real-world agricultural crises before they wipe out family farms?

In Karnataka’s Western Ghats—the heart of India's coffee heritage—smallholder coffee growers face devastating foliar outbreaks like Coffee Leaf Rust (Hemileia vastatrix) and Brown Eye Spot. By the time visual symptoms are identified by distant extension specialists, entire harvests are at risk.

To tackle this, we built **PlantIQ**: an end-to-end AI agronomy and direct marketplace platform designed specifically for Karnataka's coffee belt (Kodagu, Chikkamagaluru, and Hassan).

Check out the 46-second launch overview video below! 👇

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT I BUILT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. 📸 Real-Time Computer Vision Leaf Scanner
Farmers capture a leaf photo directly on their Android phone. An optimized PyTorch ResNet-50 model classifies 5 foliar states in ~20ms with 96.4% top-1 accuracy. Low-confidence scans (<75%) dynamically trigger blended multi-angle reasoning.

2. 🧠 3-Stage Hybrid RAG Advisory Engine
Standard LLMs hallucinate chemical dosages. We engineered a deterministic 3-stage retrieval pipeline across Central Coffee Research Institute (CCRI) agronomy handbooks:
• Stage 1: Dense semantic vector search via BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5 & FAISS (<1ms).
• Stage 2: Sparse BM25Okapi lexical retrieval for exact fungicide formulations (Bordeaux mixture 1%, Bayleton 25 WP, Hexaconazole).
• Stage 3: Cross-encoder reranking (ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2) with sigmoid confidence gating, synthesized into plain-language actionable advice via Gemini 2.5 Flash.

3. 📍 GPS-Conditioned Microclimate Telemetry
The app queries real-time agroforestry microclimate signals (elevation, temperature, relative humidity, precipitation) from Open-Meteo via GPS. Fungicide application schedules automatically adapt to local humidity thresholds and monsoon windows.

4. 🎙️ Multimodal Bilingual Chatbot with Persistent Memory
Conversations work seamlessly in Native Kannada Script (ಕನ್ನಡ ಲಿಪಿ) and English with voice input (Gemini Audio & Groq Whisper). SQLite session memory maintains multi-turn diagnostic context across app restarts.

5. ☕ Direct Farm-to-Buyer Marketplace
Farmers list parchment and cherry lots directly with GPS pins and photo galleries. Buyers contact growers via 1-tap phone calls or WhatsApp, with zero middlemen and zero commission fees.

## 🚀 PlantIQ: AI-Powered Coffee Agronomy & Direct Marketplace Platform

> **Portfolio Launch Video:** [`brag.mp4`](./brag-output/brag.mp4) (46s overview)

### What I Built
PlantIQ is a production-engineered mobile platform built for coffee farmers in Karnataka, India, combining deep learning diagnostics, localized hybrid RAG advisory, bilingual voice assistance, and direct-to-consumer crop commerce.

- **Computer Vision Foliar Diagnostics:** PyTorch ResNet-50 classifier evaluating 5 leaf states (Coffee Leaf Rust, Brown Eye Spot, Leaf Miner, Phoma Blight, Healthy) with **96.4% Top-1 Accuracy** in ~20ms inference time.
- **3-Stage Hybrid RAG Architecture:** 
  - Dense retrieval: FAISS + BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5 (768d)
  - Sparse retrieval: BM25Okapi inverted index for exact chemical compounds
  - Reranking: ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2 cross-encoder with sigmoid probability calibration
  - Synthesis: Gemini 2.5 Flash (failover to Llama 3.3 70B on Groq)
- **Microclimate Risk Context:** Dynamic GPS ingestion linking Open-Meteo elevation and humidity data directly into advisory synthesis.
- **Bilingual Voice Chatbot:** Kannada (`ಕನ್ನಡ`) & English natural conversation with Gemini Audio STT and persistent SQLite multi-turn memory.
- **Zero-Middleman Marketplace:** Direct peer-to-peer crop listing with 1-tap phone dialer, WhatsApp messaging, and Google Maps farm navigation.
